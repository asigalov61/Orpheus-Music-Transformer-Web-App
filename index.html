Best Practices
  • Good prime seed MIDI is everything — trim to an exact or approximate musical phrase
  • 30sec–1min (1024–1536 tokens) run time is ideal
  • Remove excessive instruments — 4–5 most pronounced work best
  • Don't be discouraged by generated continuations — sometimes several tries are needed
  • Don't strive for perfection — try to have fun and enjoy the music
